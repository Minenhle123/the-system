How it works

You set quests for your real life. Clearing them earns XP, gold and attribute growth. Missing a daily quest costs you. Everything below is the actual rule the app runs on, with the actual numbers.

01

The six attributes

Every quest trains exactly one attribute. All six start at 10. They only move when you clear a quest that trains them, or when you spend a point from levelling up.

AttributeTrained by
STR · StrengthResistance: push-ups, lifting, carrying load.
VIT · VitalityRecovery: sleep, water, food, rest days kept honestly.
AGI · AgilityMovement: running, mobility, sport.
INT · IntelligenceFocused work: study, deep work, building skills.
PER · PerceptionAttention: reading, journaling, noticing.
WIL · WillpowerDiscipline: quiet time, cold starts, the hard thing first.

Clearing a quest pays attribute XP to its attribute. When that reaches the cost of the next point, the attribute goes up by one and the leftover carries over. Each point costs about 8.5% more than the last:

The curve is deliberate. Getting from 10 to 11 takes one hard quest. Getting from 29 to 30 takes five.

03

Quests

Daily quests repeat every day and are judged at midnight. Side quests are one-offs with no penalty. Both pay by difficulty:

DifficultyXPGoldAttribute XP
Easy15510
Normal351222
Hard702845
Brutal1406090

A quest can have a target, such as 50 reps or 20 pages. Its button steps towards the target: +1 for targets under 20, +5 under 100, +10 above. Reaching the target clears it, or you can press COMPLETE at any time. UNDO returns the quest's rewards if you tapped by mistake.

Be honest about difficulty. The numbers only mean something if a Hard quest is actually hard for you.

04

The daily cycle and the penalty

Daily quests reset at local midnight on your device.

  • Clear every daily quest in a day: a bonus of +60 XP and +25 gold, and your streak goes up by one.
  • Leave any unfinished at midnight: −25 XP for each one, and your streak resets to zero.
  • If you are away for several days, the penalty is applied once, for the dailies that were open when you left, not once per day.

Rest passes are the only way out. Buy one in the shop for 150 gold. If a daily quest goes unfinished while you hold one, the System spends it automatically: no XP is lost and your streak holds. You have to earn the gold first, and that is the point.

05

Gates

A gate is a long goal: reading twelve books, training for a 10K. You give it a number of sessions, which becomes the boss's health. Each STRIKE +1 logs a session and drains the bar. −1 corrects a mistake. Bring the health to zero and the gate pays out by rank:

06

Gold and the shop

Gold comes from quests, the daily bonus and cleared gates. You spend it in the shop on rewards you define, at prices you set: an episode of something good, a takeaway night, gear for a hobby. Price each one by what it is worth to you. A cheap reward you never earn is better than an expensive one you buy without thinking.

The rest pass is the one item with an effect in the app. Everything else is a promise you make to yourself, kept with gold.

09

Where your data lives

Everything is stored in your browser, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. There is no account, so there is nothing to log in to and nothing to recover. That makes your save file important.

  • Save to file (Settings) downloads the-system-save.json. Open it in any text editor and the first lines tell you where you stand: name, level, rank, streak and attributes.
  • Load file restores a save. A file that is not a valid save is rejected and your current progress is left alone.
  • In Chrome and Edge on a computer you can link a save file. The System then writes to it after every change and reads it on your next visit, keeping whichever is newer.

Moving to a new phone

  1. On the old device: Settings → Save to file. Send the file to yourself however you like.
  2. On the new device: open the app and choose Load an existing save file on the first screen, or Settings → Load file.

Clearing your browser's site data erases your progress unless you have a save file. Keep one.

10

Install (optional)

THE SYSTEM is a website, and it works fully in a browser tab. You can also put it on your home screen, where it opens full screen and works offline. It is the same app either way.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. After your first cleared quest, the app offers an Install button once.
  2. Or open Chrome's menu (⋮) and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or use the browser menu → Install THE SYSTEM.

Installed or not, your progress lives in the browser it was made in. An installed app on iPhone keeps its own storage, separate from Safari, so move progress across with a save file.
